simply gain, a free gain plugin by maxximum displacement
=========================================================

What's in this folder
---------------------
  simply gain.component   the Audio Unit, for Logic Pro and GarageBand
  simply gain.vst3        the VST3, for Ableton Live, Reaper and most other DAWs
  Licences                the licences for the plugin, its fonts and its libraries

It works on macOS 11 or later, on both Intel and Apple Silicon Macs.


Installing it for Logic (about two minutes)
-------------------------------------------
1. Quit Logic.

2. In Finder, choose Go > Go to Folder..., paste this, and press Return:

       ~/Library/Audio/Plug-Ins/Components

3. Drag "simply gain.component" into that folder.

4. Tell macOS the plugin is safe. macOS blocks plugins downloaded from the
   internet unless Apple has checked them, and this one hasn't been yet.
   Open Terminal (in Applications > Utilities), paste this line, and press
   Return:

       xattr -dr com.apple.quarantine ~/Library/Audio/Plug-Ins/Components/"simply gain.component"

   Nothing is printed when it works.

5. Open Logic. It checks new plugins as it starts. You'll find this one in
   any channel strip's Audio FX slot, under:

       Audio Units > Maxximum Displacement > simply gain


For other DAWs
--------------
Do the same with "simply gain.vst3", in this folder instead:

       ~/Library/Audio/Plug-Ins/VST3

and with this line in Terminal:

       xattr -dr com.apple.quarantine ~/Library/Audio/Plug-Ins/VST3/"simply gain.vst3"


If Logic says it failed, or it doesn't appear
---------------------------------------------
Check step 4 was done. Then in Logic, choose Logic Pro > Settings >
Plug-in Manager, find "simply gain", select it, and click
"Reset & Rescan Selection".
