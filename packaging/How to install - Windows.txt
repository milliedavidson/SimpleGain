simply gain, a free gain plugin by maxximum displacement
=========================================================

What's in this folder
---------------------
  simply gain.vst3    the VST3 plugin, for Ableton Live, Reaper, FL Studio and most other DAWs
  simply gain.exe     the standalone app, to try it without a DAW
  Licences            the licences for the plugin, its fonts and its libraries

It works on 64-bit Windows 10 or later.


Before you unzip (30 seconds)
-----------------------------
Windows marks files from the internet as untrusted, and passes that on to everything inside a zip.
Unblock the zip first:

1. Right-click the .zip file and choose Properties.
2. At the bottom of the General tab, tick "Unblock", then click OK.
3. Now unzip it.

(If you can't see "Unblock", the file is already fine.)


Installing the plugin (about two minutes)
-----------------------------------------
1. Quit your DAW.

2. Copy the whole "simply gain.vst3" folder into:

       C:\Program Files\Common Files\VST3

   Windows will ask for permission to copy there. Choose Continue.

3. Open your DAW and rescan your plugins if it doesn't show up straight away.
   You'll find it under "Maxximum Displacement".


The standalone app
------------------
Double-click "simply gain.exe". It has its own Audio settings under Options.

Windows may say "Windows protected your computer", because this app hasn't been
signed with a paid certificate yet. Click "More info", then "Run anyway".


If it doesn't appear
--------------------
Check you copied the whole "simply gain.vst3" folder, not just something inside it, and
that you unblocked the zip before unzipping. Then rescan plugins in your DAW.
